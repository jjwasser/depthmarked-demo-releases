DEPTHMARKED: THE HUNT BELOW - WINDOWS DEMO 1.0.0-beta.demo.6

Extract the complete ZIP into a separate folder and run Depthmarked.Game.exe.
Keep Content beside the executable. Windows x64 runtime is included.
Choose SOLO DEMO for account-free offline play; this limited first dungeon has no expiry.
After choosing a hero, wait for dungeon preparation to finish.

For online Hunted choose the online option, then PLAY ONLINE AS GUEST.
No email or password is required. The server issues a demo-only identity and secret,
saved in Windows Credential Manager for this Windows user. Removing it can lose
online access and progress; recovery/account linking is not available yet.
Hunted needs two or three adventurers and one connected human Dungeon Master.
The pilot is capped at eight connected people in two rooms, with no instant-match promise.
Try either role or arrange a session with friends. Both roles are limited to the first dungeon.
If online service is unavailable or full, use PLAY OFFLINE (F4 / controller Y).
Online availability depends on capacity and the monthly free-tier bandwidth guard.

This demo cannot load later dungeons or full-game saves. Its saves and credentials
are separate from the full game. No full-game purchase or newsletter is required.
The demo update feed is not active; use the versioned GitHub release for new builds.

Instructions and known issues: https://github.com/jjwasser/depthmarked-demo-releases
Optional PUBLIC feedback (GitHub sign-in required):
