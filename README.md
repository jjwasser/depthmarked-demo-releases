# Depthmarked: The Hunt Below — Windows demo

A free Windows demo playtest from Reckoning Studios, with offline solo and limited guest online Hunted play. Explore the opening dungeon and tell us what works, what confuses you, and what stops you playing.

**Open solo and guest online playtest · 1.0.0-beta.demo.6 · Windows x64**

## Download and start

**[Download the Windows demo ZIP — 1.22 GB](https://github.com/jjwasser/depthmarked-demo-releases/releases/download/v1.0.0-beta.demo.6/Depthmarked-Demo-1.0.0-beta.demo.6-win-x64.zip)**

1. Allow about **4 GB of free space** for the download and extraction. Hardware requirements are still being measured in this pilot.
2. Extract the **entire ZIP** into a new folder. Keep `Content` beside the executable.
3. Open **`Depthmarked.Game.exe`**, then choose **Solo Demo → New Adventure**.
4. Wait for the six heroes to finish preparing, choose your hero, then wait for **Preparing the dungeon** to finish.

The Windows runtime is included. Solo play needs no game account or internet connection after downloading. Use the game executable for this pilot; the optional updater's public feed is not active yet.

## What is available

- The first dungeon, six playable heroes, its guardian, and a demo completion screen. Solo remains playable offline; it does not expire.
- Two loading stages: the hero roster before browsing, and the dungeon package after choosing a hero. Please report the loading time you observe.
- Local demo saves, separate from the full game.

## Play online as a guest

Use **beta.demo.6** for online play. From the adventure menu choose the online/Hunted option, then **Play online as guest**. No email, password or purchase is required. Windows Credential Manager retains the server-issued demo identity and secret for this Windows user. Removing that credential can lose online access and progress; recovery and account linking are not available yet.

Hunted needs **two or three adventurers and one connected human Dungeon Master**. Choose either role, create/join a room with friends, or try matchmaking. The pilot has **two rooms and at most eight connected players**. A lobby may be waiting for the other role; an immediate match is not promised. Online play has the same first-dungeon boundary as solo. It does not join full-game rooms or use full-game accounts.

If the service is full or unavailable, select **Play offline** (F4 / controller Y). Offline solo remains playable without expiry. Online availability depends on capacity and the shared monthly bandwidth guard. Guest creation is limited to prevent abuse; resuming a saved guest avoids creating another account. Disconnected expedition slots may be reserved briefly for reconnect.

Online report submission and automatic updates are not active. Use the feedback links below and download a new version from GitHub when announced.

This is an **open playtest**: anyone can download and try the limited demo. Our first goal is feedback from five players, followed by improvements and recruitment toward 100 first plays. Horror encounters and sudden sounds are present; bugs and rough edges are expected.
