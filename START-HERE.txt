DEPTHMARKED: THE HUNT BELOW - OPEN SOLO PLAYTEST
Reckoning Studios | 1.0.0-beta.demo.5 | Windows x64
Anyone can download and play. No invitation or tester code is required.

Download and instructions:
https://github.com/jjwasser/depthmarked-demo-releases

GET STARTED
1. Download Depthmarked-Demo-1.0.0-beta.demo.5-win-x64.zip (about 1.22 GB).
   Allow about 4 GB free for the ZIP and extraction.
2. Extract the WHOLE ZIP into a new folder. Keep Content beside the executable.
3. Open Depthmarked.Game.exe, then choose SOLO DEMO -> NEW ADVENTURE.
   No game account, sign-in, updater check, SDK, or developer tools are needed.
4. Wait for all six heroes, browse them, and choose one.
5. Wait for PREPARING THE DUNGEON to finish before play begins.

The first dungeon remains playable offline without an expiry date.
Online Hunted, registration, online reports, and the update feed are not active.
Fictional horror gameplay includes encounters and sudden sounds.

WHAT TO TRY
- A fresh download/extraction/launch and first dungeon entry.
- All six hero previews; note approximate hero/dungeon loading times.
- Normal movement, combat, light, inventory, and on-screen controls.
- Save to a new slot and resume, if available.
- Guardian, demo completion, and replay, if you have time.
A first 20-30 minute session is useful; unfinished attempts also help.
